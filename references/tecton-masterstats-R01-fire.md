## How Stats Feed HICE Scoring

The 8 stats per card map to the three ICE axes:

- **I-Axis (Intelligence)** ← COG (Cognition Score): Pattern recognition, understanding the science behind fire phenomena
- **E-Axis (Emotional)** ← SOC (Social Reach) + CTRL (Control Rating): Social awareness, empathy, ability to manage/control
- **C-Axis (Creative)** ← TRD (Trade Value) + ISI (Infrastructure Significance): Creative application, building value, infrastructure contribution

Physical stats (AGE, HEAT, SCALE) provide context and gameplay mechanics (card battles, comparisons) but feed indirectly into the HICE score through the player's ability to recognise patterns and make connections.
